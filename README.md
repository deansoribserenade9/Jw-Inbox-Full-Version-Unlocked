<a id="overview"></a>

JW Inbox is a straightforward application that keeps you informed about new emails in your webmail accounts. It uses the POP3 and IMAP4 protocols to check for incoming messages and notifies you via a small system tray icon. This tool is especially useful for users who prefer not to constantly check their email through a web browser. Compared to typical email notification solutions, JW Inbox offers a simple and effective way to manage your email alerts on your desktop.
